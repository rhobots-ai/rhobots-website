The page hero: `eyebrow`, `title`, `subtitle`, `actions`, and an optional `media` column that turns it into two columns at `lg`. `align="center"` centres the copy.

```jsx
<Hero
  eyebrow="Platform"
  title="Enterprise AI that works with what you have"
  subtitle="Six products that plug into the systems you already run."
  actions={<Button>Request a demo</Button>}
/>
```
