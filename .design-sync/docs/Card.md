A bordered content block. `tone="muted"` tints it for use on a white section; `padding` sets the interior scale. Pass `to` to make the whole card a link with a hover affordance.

```jsx
<Card tone="muted" padding="lg">
  <Heading level={3}>Extract</Heading>
  <p className="mt-3 font-body text-sm text-on-surface-variant">Structured records from any document.</p>
</Card>
```
